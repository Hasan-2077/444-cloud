# Published Threshold Sources

## Rain

Source:
NOAA JetStream Weather Glossary / Federal Meteorological Standards

Published guidance:
- Light rain: up to approximately 2.5 mm/hour
- Moderate rain: approximately 2.8 to 7.6 mm/hour
- Heavy rain: greater than approximately 7.6 mm/hour

Project rule:
- No: 0 mm/hour with low precipitation probability
- Mild: > 0 through 7.6 mm/hour
- Heavy: > 7.6 mm/hour

The project combines the published Light and Moderate categories into the required Mild category.

## Snow

Source:
NOAA/National Weather Service heavy-snow guidance

Published guidance:
Heavy snow generally means approximately 10 cm or more accumulated within 12 hours, although warning criteria can vary by region.

Project rule:
- No: 0 cm during the six-hour report period
- Mild: > 0 and < 10 cm
- Heavy: >= 10 cm during the six-hour report period

The Heavy threshold is intentionally conservative. Reaching the published 12-hour heavy-snow amount within only six hours clearly qualifies as significant snowfall.
